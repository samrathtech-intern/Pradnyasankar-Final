# Final Design Refinement

This revision addresses the visual repetition and missing-image issues identified during review.

## Reworked sections

- Hero simplified to one campaign image per slide with no secondary floating photograph or glass text card.
- Trust content reduced to a compact continuous information ribbon.
- Wellness-focus discovery changed from a generic 2x4 card grid to a unified selected-story stage with an integrated visual selector.
- Featured products changed from a repeated catalogue grid into an animated product spotlight with a nearby collection selector.
- Product formats changed to a restrained product shelf with neutral, consistent image stages.
- Daily routines changed to a morning-to-evening visual timeline, clearly distinct from wellness-focus discovery.
- Wellness sets now use separate image and solid text panels for reliable contrast.
- Transparency section now shows the product-label image clearly and keeps selected information in a dedicated dark panel.
- Greens Nutrition is presented as a visible featured ingredient with object-contain image handling.
- Community storytelling changed to an image-led bento layout.
- Business enquiry section now includes a visible product composition, product flatlay and a supporting business-information panel.

## Content and compliance

- No AI-generation labels or badges are displayed.
- No placeholder pricing, fabricated ratings or fabricated testimonials were added.
- Medical diagnosis, prescription and treatment language remains excluded.
